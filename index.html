HOW TO PLAY
1. Press START / NEXT to bowl. 2. Choose LEFT, STRAIGHT or RIGHT. 3. Press SWING as the ball reaches the lower crease. Better timing means stronger shots. Fielders can stop ground shots or catch aerial shots; shots reaching the rope score four, and clearing it score six.
KEYBOARD: ← / A = left · ↑ / W = straight · → / D = right · SPACE = swing · ENTER = start/next · P = pause.
TOUCH: Use the on-screen buttons. You can also tap the field to swing during a delivery.
