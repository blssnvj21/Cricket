HOW TO PLAY
Start the innings. The bowler sends a delivery towards your wicket. Pick a shot direction and swing as the ball reaches the batting crease. Timing, direction and field placement affect the result. Lofted shots can be caught; ground shots can be stopped; the boundary gives four and a shot clearing it gives six. Five wickets end the innings. Overs contain six legal balls.
